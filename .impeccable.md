## Design Context

### Users
The creator and a small group of early testers use Trellis for sustained thinking with agents (see PRODUCT.md).

### Brand Personality
Calm, papery, scholarly. The incumbent interface and DESIGN.md supply the design direction.

### Aesthetic Direction
The Naturalist's Field Desk: warm paper, compact scalable type, outlined controls, and semantic proposal borders. Reuse existing tokens and typography.

### Design Principles
- Keep the graph primary and conversations attached to thoughts.
- Distinguish discussion, proposed content, and ratified state visibly.
- Make agent context inspectable.
- Preserve drafts and continuity when panels close.
