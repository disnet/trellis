what would it really mean to think with an agent? by think I'm meaning something along the lines of a PKB in the lineage of roam reasearch, obsidian, etc. where the goal is to have a body if reified concepts that you can reference and grow over time.

the current interfaces we have for "thinking with agents" feels very stuck in previous paradigms. the dominant approach is the chatbot, even when the chatbot can go off and perform actions its still presented to the user as a chatlog. this is a problem for the human to keep things in context as the chat grows to any length.

we have interfaces that were built up prior to the agent world like obsidian and notion but their agent integrations feel bolted on. what could it look like if we build a thinking system design from the ground up around agents?

I think the core requirements are something along the lines of:

- assume and support non-linear thinking on the part of the human
- don't drown them in a wall of text they need to read
- present and allow manipulation of chunks
  - something like obsidian pages but can we do better?
